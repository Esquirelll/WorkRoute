# WorkRoute: 5-minute demo script

Target: **4 min 45 s** of speaking, leaving a little room. About 660 spoken words. Rehearse it twice with a timer.
If you run long, cut the employer part (4:20–4:40). If you run short, open "Why this match?" on a second job.

## Before you record (10 minutes)

1. Start the site: `npm run build` then `npm run preview` (or use your GitHub Pages link). Use a fresh browser window, hide the bookmarks bar, close other tabs, turn on Do Not Disturb.
2. Make an employer account once, in advance: Sign up, choose **Employer**, name "Demo Employer", email `demo-employer@example.com`, any 8+ character password. On the Employer page enter workplace name "Made-up Works", area "Pasig", set 2 or 3 items to Confirmed, press **Save profile**. Log out.
3. Click **Try demo persona** once, then open Account and privacy and press **Reset the demo persona**, so Maria starts clean. Log out.
4. Browser zoom 110–125% so text is readable on video. Window about 1280 × 720 or full HD.
5. Make sure your system's "Reduce motion" setting is OFF (the animations are part of the story).
6. Do one silent dry run of the clicks below.

## The script

| Time | On screen | What you say |
|---|---|---|
| **0:00–0:35 THE PROBLEM** | Home page. Scroll slowly, then stop on the example card. | "If you are a person with a disability in Metro Manila, finding a job means asking two questions, not one. Can I **work** there? Is there a ramp or a lift, a restroom I can use, tools my screen reader can read? And can I **get** there? Can I board the bus, is the station step-free, are the stops announced? A job is only a real option if both answers are yes. And if the journey fails at even one step, the whole journey fails." |
| **0:35–1:10 THE GAP** | Stay on the home page. Point at the two badges. | "Today those answers live in different places. Job platforms match people to jobs. Accessibility maps show places. I haven't found a tool that puts the workplace and the commute together, in one view, for the person looking for work. That combination is the gap." |
| **1:10–1:50 THE INVENTION** | Point at the badges, then at the three status icons in the legend later. | "WorkRoute closes that gap. Every job shows two badges side by side: **Workplace** and **Commute**. Each is confirmed, not confirmed, or not available, shown with a shape, an icon and words, never color alone. One rule runs through the whole app: **not confirmed is never shown as accessible.** If nobody has said, WorkRoute says: ask the employer. Everything you'll see is sample data: invented employers, jobs and routes. And the matching is plain written rules, no AI." |
| **1:50–2:15 DEMO: THE PERSONA** | Click **Try demo persona**. The Jobs page opens. | "Let me show it as Maria, a fictional wheelchair user. One click, no log-in. Her needs are already chosen: she uses a wheelchair and needs an accessible restroom. We never ask for a diagnosis, only what helps." |
| **2:15–2:45 THE FEED** | Slowly move the pointer: left card, center, right legend. Open "Why this match?" on Data Entry Associate. | "Here is her feed. On the left, her needs. On the right, how to read the badges. In the middle, jobs ranked by match. Data Entry Associate is 100 percent: workplace confirmed, commute confirmed. 'Why this match?' lists every need and the reason behind every badge." |
| **2:45–3:10 FILTERS** | Click Hybrid, then "Up to 45 min", then clear. Tick **Show excluded jobs**, then untick. | "I can filter, hybrid only, or travel up to forty-five minutes, and the cards glide into place. Three jobs are hidden because a need isn't available. Show excluded jobs reveals them, so nothing disappears silently." |
| **3:10–3:35 JOB DETAIL** | Open **Learning Coordinator**. Scroll to the workplace checklist. | "Learning Coordinator is 50 percent. The workplace checklist shows what the employer has stated. Captions and visual alerts are *not confirmed*, so it says: ask the employer before relying on it." |
| **3:35–4:05 THE COMMUTE** | Scroll to the stepper and let the line draw. Point at step 3. | "Now the commute, step by step. The line draws the journey: a walk, the LRT or MRT, then a bus. On step three, wheelchair boarding is not confirmed. WorkRoute shows which step affects which need. The weakest step decides." |
| **4:05–4:20 SAVE** | Click **Save**, go Back, click **Show saved jobs**. | "Maria can save this job and come back to it later." |
| **4:20–4:40 EMPLOYER + PRIVACY** | Log out. Log in as `demo-employer@example.com`. Show the form and the preview. Then Account and privacy, stored list. | "On the other side, an employer describes their workplace, and a preview shows how a job seeker would see it. Everything starts as not confirmed, so an employer must choose confirmed on purpose. And for privacy, this page lists exactly what is stored in your browser, and lets you clear it or delete your account." |
| **4:40–5:00 GOALS AND CLOSE** | Method page, scroll to the Global goals section. | "WorkRoute supports three Sustainable Development Goals. SDG 8, decent work: its target on employment for all names persons with disabilities. SDG 10, reduced inequalities, by making accessibility visible and honest about what is unknown. And SDG 11, sustainable cities, because a job you can't reach is not an opportunity. This is a prototype with sample data. The next step would be verified information from employers and transport operators. Thank you." |

## Notes

- **Numbers:** the script uses no statistics, on purpose. If you want one, use a figure from a source you can name (for example the Philippine Statistics Authority or the National Council on Disability Affairs), and say where it comes from on camera.
- **If something goes wrong on camera:** Account and privacy, "Reset the demo persona", then start the persona again. Maria always restarts from the same state.
- **Keyboard moment (optional, 10 seconds):** press Tab once to show the skip link and the thick focus outline: "Everything works with a keyboard alone."
